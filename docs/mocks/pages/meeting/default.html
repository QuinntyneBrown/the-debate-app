Universal basic income

Tue 13 Oct · 7:00 pm–8:30 pm

Edit

This house would introduce a universal basic income. Priya Nair proposes; Samir Haddad opposes. Two speakers a side, eight minutes each, then open floor.

Order of play

  1. Welcome and house rules (5 min)
  2. Proposition, Priya Nair (8 min)
  3. Opposition, Samir Haddad (8 min)
  4. Second speakers, Lena Fischer and Tom Okafor (8 min each)
  5. Open floor (20 min)
  6. Summaries and vote (10 min)

Before the meeting

Read the two-page brief Priya circulated on Friday.

Fourteen members attended. The motion carried 9–5 after a close open floor.

Highlights

  • Priya's strongest point was the administrative saving from replacing means-tested benefits.
  • Samir's objection about inflation drew the most questions from the floor.
  • Lena proposed a pilot-scheme compromise that several members said changed their vote.

Actions

  • Tom to post the reading list for the AI motion by Wednesday.
  • Jordan to confirm the room for 27 October.
