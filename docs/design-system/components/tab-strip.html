Tab strip

Shows one of a meeting's Abstract, Agenda or Minutes at a time, with arrow, Home and End keys.

Overview

The tab strip shows one section of a meeting at a time: Abstract, Agenda or Minutes. Use it when sections are peers of one record and the reader wants one on screen. It replaces the ce-fancy-tabs custom element (L2-028) with native buttons and the WAI-ARIA Tabs pattern.

  • Use it on the meeting page (read) and on the meeting create and edit forms (write), always with the same three tabs in the same order.
  • Do not use it for navigation between pages; use links in the top bar. Do not use it for two or fewer sections.

Anatomy

  1. Tab listrole="tablist" with an aria-label; a pill track on --color-bg-subtle that scrolls sideways when too narrow.
  2. Selected tabrole="tab" button with aria-selected="true", raised on --color-bg-surface with --shadow-1.
  3. Unselected tabaria-selected="false" and tabindex="-1"; muted label. Panels (role="tabpanel", tabindex="0") follow the list and unselected ones carry hidden.

Variants

One variant. The strip is identical for reading and editing; only the panel content changes (prose to read, a rich-text editor to write).

With an empty-section dot

A trailing dot marks an empty section; its meaning is repeated for screen readers in visually hidden text.

Sizes

One size: --control-height-sm tall (36px) with --space-4 side padding. Keep the strip at least --space-2 from neighbouring controls. Tab labels never wrap.

States

Real pages get hover and focus from :hover and :focus-visible; they are forced here with data-state for review. Selection is aria-selected.

DefaultHoverFocusSelectedSelected and focus

Both themes

  • Disabled does not apply: every section always exists, so no tab is ever disabled.
  • Loading and error: the strip stays as it is; the panel shows its own skeleton or alert.

Responsive behaviour

  • The strip is width: fit-content with max-width: 100% and scrolls horizontally inside its own track when the tabs do not fit; the page never scrolls sideways.
  • Three tabs fit at 360px, so no breakpoint changes the layout.
  • Panels are fluid; the editor or prose inside fills the content column.

Theming

The strip reads semantic tokens only: --color-bg-subtle (track), --color-bg-surface (selected), --color-fg-muted and --color-fg-default (labels) and --shadow-1. The dark theme swaps the values with no extra rules. Selection is shown by fill, shadow and stronger label together, not colour alone.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA Tabs pattern with automatic activation: the list is role="tablist", each button role="tab" with aria-controls, each panel role="tabpanel" with aria-labelledby. Only the selected tab is in the tab order (roving tabindex: 0 on the selected tab, -1 on the others).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabEnters the strip on the selected tab, then moves on to the panel.
→ / ↓Selects and focuses the next tab; wraps from last to first.
← / ↑Selects and focuses the previous tab; wraps from first to last.
Home / EndSelects the first or last tab.
Enter, SpaceActivates the focused tab (already selected by the arrows).

Focus

The shared ring on :focus-visible: --focus-ring-width of --color-focus-ring at --focus-ring-offset. Focus stays on the tab after selection; it does not jump into the panel.

Labelling

The tab list has an aria-label ("Meeting sections"); each tab's name is its visible text. The decorative dot is aria-hidden and replaced by visually hidden text.

Contrast

Unselected label on track
Selected label on pill
Hover label on track
Focus ring on surface

Motion

Background and colour change over --duration-fast with --ease-standard; reduced motion zeroes the duration.

Touch

Tabs are 36px tall, above the 24px minimum, inside a track padded by --space-1.

Content

  • Labels are single nouns in sentence case: "Abstract", "Agenda", "Minutes". Same order everywhere.
  • No counts or icons in the label; the empty-section dot is the only adornment.
  • Open Minutes first for a past meeting and Abstract first for an upcoming one.
