Menu

The account menu opens from the avatar in the top bar. It says who is signed in and offers the one thing you do to the account: sign out.

Overview

The Debate App has one menu: the account menu. A button.avatar in the top bar opens a .menu list anchored under it. The only item is a link to the sign-in page, "Sign out".

  • Use it for account actions that do not belong on any one page.
  • Do not use it for the main areas of the app: Meetings and New meeting live in the top bar (see top bar).
  • Do not use it for row actions such as Edit and Delete; those are icon buttons on the meeting row.

Anatomy

  1. Triggerbutton.avatar with aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded; 32px in the top bar, with the initial "J".
  2. Identity headerWho is signed in: "Jordan Reyes" and "@admin". Not an item: role="none", never focused.
  3. Divider.menu__divider, aria-hidden="true"; separates the header from the action.
  4. Item.menu__item: icon plus label ("Sign out"), 36px tall, full width.

Variants

Not applicable: there is one account menu, for the signed-in user. It does not change by role; every signed-in person sees the same two parts.

Responsive behaviour

  • The menu is the same at every width: a 14rem panel below the avatar, right edge on --layout-margin. At 360px it takes about two thirds of the width and never needs to become a bottom sheet.
  • It sits on --z-dropdown, below dialogs and toasts and above the sticky top bar.
  • A long display name in the header wraps; the item never wraps because its label is two words.

Theming

The panel reads --color-bg-surface-raised, --color-border-default and --shadow-3; items read --color-fg-default and --color-bg-subtle for hover and focus. In dark the raised surface is lighter than the page, so elevation shows without relying on the shadow. The trigger's hover ring is --color-border-strong.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA APG Menu Button pattern. The trigger is a native <button> with aria-haspopup="menu", aria-expanded and aria-controls pointing at the menu. The list is role="menu", each <li> is role="none" and each link is role="menuitem". Focus inside the menu is roving: only the current item has tabindex="0".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter, Space, ↓ on the triggerOpens the menu and focuses the first item.
↑ on the triggerOpens the menu and focuses the last item (Sign out).
↓ / ↑Moves to the next or previous item, wrapping at the ends. The header and dividers are skipped.
Home / EndMoves to the first or last item.
EnterFollows the focused item and closes the menu.
EscCloses the menu and returns focus to the avatar.
TabCloses the menu and moves focus to the next element after the trigger.

Focus

The trigger shows the shared focus ring. Items show focus with the --color-bg-subtle hover fill plus the shared focus ring drawn inside the item (a fill alone is too faint against the raised panel), and the menu moves real DOM focus (not aria-activedescendant). A click outside closes the menu without moving focus.

Labelling

The trigger's aria-label names whose menu it is ("Account menu for Jordan Reyes"), because the visible "J" says little. The menu repeats it with aria-label. The identity header is plain text inside role="none", so it is not announced as an item; icons are aria-hidden.

Motion

The panel scales in from the top right over --duration-fast with --ease-enter. Reduced motion zeroes the duration, so it simply appears.

Touch

Items are 36px tall and span the panel's full width, well over the 24px minimum. The 32px avatar trigger is the only target in its corner of the top bar.

Content

  • The header names the person and the username on a second line: "Jordan Reyes", "@admin".
  • Items are short, sentence case: "Sign out". One icon each, from the shared icon set.
  • Sign out is always last, after a divider, so it is not hit by accident.
