Top bar

The bar across the top of every signed-in page: the brand, two links (Meetings and New meeting) from 768px, and Jordan's avatar, which opens the account menu.

Overview

Every signed-in page starts with a .topbar. It keeps the way back to the meeting list and the account menu one tap away, and it stays put while the page scrolls. The sign-in and home pages, and the "Sign in to continue" page, have no top bar; they use the auth layout or a bare container.

  • The brand ("The Debate App") links to the meeting list.
  • .topbar__nav holds two links, Meetings and New meeting, and is hidden below 768px. The Meetings page repeats New meeting as its primary action, so phones lose nothing.
  • The avatar is a button.avatar that opens the account menu (who is signed in, Sign out).
  • There is no search, tab bar, notification count or role switch: the Debate App has one signed-in person and one list.

Anatomy

  1. Brand.topbar__brand: speech-bubble icon and "The Debate App" in --text-h4; a link to the meeting list.
  2. Navnav.topbar__nav of two .nav-links; the current page has aria-current="page". Hidden below 768px.
  3. Spacer.topbar__spacer pushes the avatar to the right edge.
  4. Avatarbutton.avatar (small, sky, initial "J") that opens the account menu.
  5. Capsuleheader.topbar: a pill (--radius-full) floating --space-2 below the top of the screen, as wide as the page container less its margins, translucent surface with a background blur, hairline border all round and --shadow-2.

Variants

One bar for the one kind of signed-in person; only the current link changes between pages.

Sizes

The bar is always --layout-topbar-height (56px) tall; only its contents change with width.

States

The bar has no state of its own; its links and avatar do. Hover and focus are forced with data-state; current uses the real aria-current="page".

Both themes

The Debate App
The Debate App
  • Current nav link: --color-accent-subtle fill and --color-fg-accent text, plus aria-current="page" for screen readers.
  • Avatar open: aria-expanded="true" while the account menu is showing; the hover ring stays so the trigger is visibly tied to the menu.
  • Nav links carry no count or badge; the Debate App has nothing to count.

Responsive behaviour

  • Base (phones): brand, spacer, avatar. Side padding is --layout-margin, so the bar lines up with page content at every width.
  • From --layout-breakpoint-md (768px) .topbar__nav appears (display: flex). Below 768px there is no replacement nav: New meeting is also the primary action in the Meetings page header, and the brand returns to the list.
  • The bar is a floating capsule: position: sticky; top: var(--space-2) at --z-sticky, with --space-2 above it and auto side margins. Its width is min(--layout-container-max, 100%) less the page margin on each side, so it lines up with the content column at every width. Sticky (not fixed) keeps it in flow, so nothing ever starts hidden under it. scroll-padding-top on html equals its height plus --space-4, which covers the 8px offset, so in-page links and focused fields are never hidden under it.
  • Nothing truncates: the brand and two links are short and the avatar shows one initial.

Theming

The bar is --color-bg-surface at 90% (color-mix) over a 12px backdrop blur, with a hairline --color-border-default all round and --shadow-2, so it reads as floating above the page in both themes; content scrolling under it shows through faintly. Text contrast is measured against the opaque surface: the blur and 90% fill keep anything behind it from lowering the ratio noticeably. Nav links read --color-fg-muted, --color-bg-subtle and the accent pair; the avatar reads the sky swatch of the avatar palette with --color-fg-on-cover. The dark theme changes only those semantic tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <header> (the page's banner landmark) holding a native <nav aria-label="Primary"> of links. Current page is aria-current="page". The avatar is a Menu Button (APG): aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabSkip link, brand, nav links (768px and up), avatar, then the page.
EnterFollows the brand or a nav link.
Enter, Space, ↓ on the avatarOpens the account menu and moves focus to its first item.
Esc in the menuCloses the menu and returns focus to the avatar.

Focus

The shared 2px --color-focus-ring with 2px offset on every link and the avatar. The bar never covers focus: scroll-padding-top reserves its height (WCAG 2.4.11).

Labelling

The avatar shows only "J", so its aria-label says whose menu it opens: "Account menu for Jordan Reyes". The brand's icon is aria-hidden; the words "The Debate App" name the link. The nav is aria-label="Primary".

Motion

Nav links fade their background and colour over --duration-fast. Nothing else moves; the bar does not hide on scroll. Reduced motion zeroes the fade.

Touch

Nav links are --target-comfortable (44px) tall. The small avatar is 32px (--size-avatar-sm), above the 24px minimum, with the spacer keeping it well clear of other targets.

Content

  • The brand is always "The Debate App"; no page titles in the bar (the page header has the <h1>).
  • Nav labels are sentence case, in this order: Meetings, New meeting. The order never changes between pages.
  • The avatar shows the initial of the signed-in person: "J" for Jordan Reyes.

Drift found in the mocks:

  • The bar markup is pasted into 40 files, each with its own copy of the brand icon and links; one edit has to be made 40 times.
  • Below 768px the nav hides and no mock provides a phone replacement. New meeting is still reachable from the Meetings page header, but not from the meeting, edit or error pages.
  • The not-found, offline and server-error pages keep the signed-in bar even when the problem is the connection; their only avatar action (the account menu) will fail offline.

Fixed earlier in the kit: the bottom border was a raw 1px and is now --border-width-hairline all round the capsule; button.avatar carries its own border reset and hover ring.
