Form layout

The parts that hold a form together: the meeting header strip, the row of actions, the error summary at the top, the danger zone below, and what everything does while it saves.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <form>

Overview

Form layout is a handful of small classes plus one rule for the submitting state. The whole composition (field order, validation timing) is in the forms pattern; the Save button and status of the meeting forms are the save bar.

  • .meeting-form__header is the compact strip at the top of the meeting form: Name wide, then Date, Start and End side by side. Name is a text field; the other three are native date and time pickers.
  • .form-actions ends a form that has its actions at the bottom: an end-aligned, wrapping row with the primary last. (Meeting forms put Save in the page header instead.)
  • .form-summary is an alert (.alert.alert--danger) at the top of a form that failed. It counts the problems and links each to its field.
  • .danger-zone sits after the form on Edit meeting. It holds the one destructive action and says what it does; the button opens a confirmation dialog.
  • Submitting: the pressed button gets aria-busy="true", and text, date and time fields become readonly.

Anatomy

  1. Error summary.alert.alert--danger.form-summary, role="alert", tabindex="-1"; a thicker --color-danger-solid left edge, one link per problem.
  2. NameThe wide first column of .meeting-form__header, minmax(0, 2fr).
  3. Date, Start, EndThree equal columns, minmax(7rem, 1fr), --space-4 apart; native pickers.

Date and time inputs

Date and time are native type="date" and type="time" inputs. They share the text field box, label and error and carry the same states (default, hover, focus, invalid, read-only, disabled); the matrix in text field states shows each one. The value is ISO and 24-hour; the browser shows it in the viewer's locale. An End before Start is the form's rule: it marks End invalid and adds a summary link.

  • Invalid: on submit, client checks run first; the summary appears at the top, takes focus, and each field shows its own error. Values stay as typed.
  • Submitting: the pressed button is disabled aria-busy="true" and says "Saving…"; text, date and time fields are readonly. Nothing moves, so there is no layout shift.
  • Failed (the server could not be reached) is not a form summary: it is an alert such as "The meeting was not saved", with the form returned to its default state.

Responsive behaviour

  • .meeting-form__header is one column under 48rem. From 48rem it becomes minmax(0, 2fr) plus three minmax(7rem, 1fr) columns with align-items: start, so a field error under one input does not push its neighbours down.
  • .form-actions wraps: if "Cancel" and the primary do not fit, the primary wraps below and stays at the end.
  • .danger-zone wraps: on phones the button drops below the explanation; when there is room they sit side by side.
  • The error summary is always the first thing in the form, so it is in view after focus moves to it on any screen size.

Theming

The summary inherits the danger alert's tokens (--color-danger-bg, --color-danger-fg, --color-danger-icon) and adds a --space-1 wide --color-danger-solid left edge. The danger zone uses --color-danger-border and --radius-lg on the page background, so it reads as a warning without filling with red. The header strip uses only --space-4 for its gap; the fields inside bring their own tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <form> with novalidate so the app's own messages replace the browser bubbles. The summary is role="alert" with tabindex="-1"; it is announced and receives focus after a failed submit (WCAG 3.3.1). The danger zone is a <section> named by its heading. The header strip is plain layout: every input has its own visible <label>, so no group role is needed.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter in a text fieldSubmits the form.
Tab through the stripName, Date, Start, End in reading order; each date or time field has several segments you move through with ← and →.
Tab after a failed submitFocus is on the summary; Tab reaches its first link.
Enter on a summary linkMoves focus to that field, which is scrolled into view below the sticky top bar.

Focus

After a failed submit, focus moves to the summary. While submitting, focus stays put: read-only fields keep it; the busy button is disabled, so when the request ends focus goes to the result (the status, the summary, or the next page). In the danger zone, the button opens a dialog whose initial focus is the safe button.

Labelling

The summary title counts the problems ("Fix 2 things to save"); each link names the problem ("End must be after start"), not just the field. Field errors are joined to their controls with aria-describedby. A busy button keeps its name and adds aria-busy="true". Date and time inputs are labelled "Date", "Start", "End" by visible labels, never by placeholder.

Motion

None of its own. The summary appears without animation; the busy button's spinner follows the button rules.

Touch

Summary links are inline text links in a list, with the list's line height giving at least 24px per row. Inputs are 44px tall; native pickers provide large popups on touch devices.

Content

  • Summary titles are short and calm: "Fix 2 things to save", "One thing to fix", "We could not sign you in".
  • Summary links name the problem: "Enter a name", "End must be after start". The field error below says the same thing, in the same words.
  • The danger zone heading names the action ("Delete this meeting") and the text says what happens ("It disappears from the list for everyone. There is no undo.").
  • Primary labels say what saving does: "Save", "Create meeting", "Sign in". Not "Submit".
