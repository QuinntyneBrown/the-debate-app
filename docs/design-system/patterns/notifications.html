Notifications

A toast reports something that just happened and then gets out of the way. A banner reports something that is still true, and stays until it is no longer true or is dismissed.

Overview

A toast reports something that just happened and then gets out of the way. A banner reports something that is still true, or news that is waiting, and stays until it is no longer true or is dismissed. Both use four statuses: success, info, warning and danger, each with an icon, a title and words, never colour alone.

  • Toast: bottom of the screen (bottom right from 640px), 8 seconds, pauses on hover and focus, newest at the bottom.
  • Banner: full width under the top bar, above the page content, one at a time.
  • Inline alert: for a problem tied to one form or dialog; see Alert.

When to use

SituationUseExample
An action finished and nothing else is neededSuccess toast"Meeting saved"
An action failed and the person can retryDanger toast with an action"We couldn't save the meeting" · Try again
The app state changed and may matterInfo toast"Signed in as Jordan Reyes"
A condition persistsBanner"You're offline. Changes can't be saved until the connection is back."
A failure inside a form or dialogInline alert"We couldn't delete it"
Something that needs a decisionDialogSee Dialogs and overlays

Structure

The toast region exists in the page from the start so assistive technology announces new toasts. Each toast has an icon, a title, one line of text, an optional action and a timer bar.

Responsive

  • Under 640px toasts are full width at the bottom; from 640px they are 24rem wide at the bottom right.
  • Banners stack text and actions on phones and centre in one row from 640px.
  • Toasts never cover the save bar or a focused control; the page keeps bottom padding while one is showing.

Accessibility

  • The toast region is role="status" with aria-live="polite"; danger banners use role="alert".
  • Toasts last 8 seconds, pause on hover and focus, and have a Dismiss control; danger toasts with an action do not time out (2.2.1).
  • Actions inside a toast are real links or buttons and are reachable by keyboard.
  • Status is icon plus title plus words, and each icon meets 3:1 on its surface in both themes.
  • Toast text is short enough to be read in the time available.

Content

  • Title says what happened in past tense: "Meeting saved", "Meeting deleted".
  • Detail names the meeting or the next step: "Universal basic income · 4:12 pm".
  • Failures say what did not happen and what is safe: "We couldn't save the meeting" / "Your changes are still in the form."
  • Offline copy is consistent: "You're offline. Changes can't be saved until the connection is back."
