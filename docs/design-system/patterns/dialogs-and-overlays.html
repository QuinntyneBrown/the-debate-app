Dialogs and overlays

The Debate App has two kinds of overlay: a modal dialog for a short decision in context, and the account menu. Anything longer is a page.

Overview

The Debate App has three overlays: a modal dialog to confirm deleting a meeting, a modal dialog to confirm leaving the editor with unsaved changes, and the account menu opened from the avatar. Anything longer than a question and two buttons is a page, not a dialog.

  • A dialog asks one question and has two buttons: the safe one and the consequence.
  • The menu lists actions for the signed-in person: who they are and Sign out.
  • Neither overlay holds a form. Editing a meeting happens on its page.

When to use

NeedUseWhy
Confirm a destructive or irreversible actionDialogThe decision must be made now, in context ("Delete meeting")
Warn before losing workDialogLeaving the editor discards typed content
Account actionsMenuSign out and who's signed in, one tap from any page
Report that something workedToastNothing to decide
Edit a meetingPageRich text and seven fields need room

Structure

A dialog is a backdrop plus a panel with a header (icon, title, optional description, close), an optional body and a footer of buttons. On phones it is a bottom sheet; from 640px it is a centred panel up to 32rem wide.

Responsive

  • Under 640px a dialog is a bottom sheet: full width, rounded top corners, footer buttons stacked with the primary action last.
  • From 640px it is centred, up to 32rem, with buttons in one row, right aligned.
  • The menu is anchored under the avatar and never wider than the viewport minus 16px.

Accessibility

  • Dialogs use role="dialog", aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby the title and aria-describedby the description.
  • Focus moves into the dialog (to the safe button for destructive prompts), is trapped inside, and returns to the control that opened it on close.
  • Escape closes the dialog (data-dismiss-on-escape) and counts as Cancel.
  • The avatar button has aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded; the menu uses role="menu" with arrow-key navigation, Escape to close and focus back on the avatar.
  • The backdrop is not focusable and does not close the dialog on click when a request is in flight.
  • Errors inside a dialog use role="alert".

Content

  • Title is the question with the thing named: "Delete “Universal basic income”?"
  • Description says the consequence once: "It disappears from the list for everyone. There's no undo."
  • Buttons name the action: "Delete meeting" and "Keep editing"/"Discard"; never "OK" or "Yes".
  • Failure text names what did not happen and the fix: "We couldn't delete it" / "Check your connection and try again."
